# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Medical residents record training cases; supervisors verify work; program directors review progress; institution admins manage their own tenant; platform super admins operate the installation and govern tenants. The repository's initial pilot targets General Surgery and Internal Medicine in Saudi Arabia. Mobile exists, while the current pilot is web first.

## Product Purpose

Make clinical training records quick to enter, easy to verify, and useful for program oversight. Keep authorization, tenant boundaries, patient-data policy, and recovery dependable across the web app, database, exports, and future offline clients.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, precise. Preserve and refine the existing Apple Health-inspired identity, as requested by the owner on 2026-09-07. The authenticated workspace is the primary design surface; public landing pages have their own content and presentation needs.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard animation, glass effects behind dense data, oversized statistics, nested panels, and inconsistent controls. These are working design constraints derived from the existing clinical workflow and the requested refinement, rather than a request for a new brand.

## Design Principles

1. Prioritize entering, finding, and verifying records over decoration.
2. Make the current tenant, permissions, saved state, and data mode clear.
3. Make related workflows behave consistently across roles and screen sizes.
4. Explain consequential changes before execution and preserve recoverability.
5. Apply tenant customization within platform policy and accessibility limits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, and readable status labels that do not depend on color. Preserve existing locale support and verify Arabic/RTL before claiming that support. These are proposed acceptance targets in the upgrade plan, not a claim that the current UI meets them.
